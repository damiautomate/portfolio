Content Repair

Fills in content sections that are missing from your live Firestore document — without touching anything that is already there. Use this instead of migrate.html, which overwrites everything.

What this does: compares your published + draft documents against the full schema in site-data.js, and adds only the keys that are missing or empty. Existing values always win. Nothing is deleted. A JSON backup downloads before any write.
