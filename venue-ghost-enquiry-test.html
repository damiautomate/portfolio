Ghost Enquiry Self-Test

Find out what really happens when a couple tries to book you.

Every day, prospects call, fill in your form, and DM you. This is a 5-minute secret test of your own venue — so you see exactly what they experience.

✓ You run it yourself · No signup to see your score

First — run the test (5 minutes)

Do these as if you were a couple who has never contacted your venue. Best done from a phone or email your team won't recognise.

Call your venue. Use a number your team doesn't have saved. Note: did someone answer? If not, what happened — voicemail, a text back, nothing?
Submit your website enquiry form (or send an email as a couple asking about a date). Note when — you'll time the reply.
DM your Instagram / Facebook with a quick "Hi, is [date] available?" — the way most couples actually reach out now.
Wait, and watch. Give it a few hours (ideally a day). Then come back and score exactly what happened below.
